Remote Work Policy
Last Updated: 2026-01-01
============================================================

## 1. Eligibility

Employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous employment and
whose role is classified as remote-eligible by their department head may apply
for a remote work arrangement.

## 2. Equipment

Employees approved for remote work will be issued a company laptop, headset,
and docking station. All issued equipment remains company property and must be
returned upon termination or role change. Employees must follow the Equipment
Loan Agreement as defined in the IT Asset Policy.

## 3. Data Security

Remote employees must use the company-provided VPN for all access to internal
systems. Connecting to public Wi-Fi without VPN is prohibited. Data handling
during remote work is governed by the Data Classification Framework — see
Section 4.

## 4. Working Hours

Remote employees are expected to be available and responsive during their
team's core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone.
Employees must attend all scheduled team meetings and flag scheduling
conflicts to their manager at least 48 hours in advance.

## 5. Performance and Accountability

Remote employees are evaluated using the same performance criteria as
on-site employees. Managers must conduct check-ins at least once per week.
Performance improvement plans for remote employees follow the standard HR
process documented in the Employee Handbook.

## 6. International Remote Work

Employees wishing to work remotely from outside their country of employment
for more than 14 consecutive days must obtain written approval from their
manager and HR. For international remote work, refer to the Global Mobility
Policy for applicable rules.

## 7. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually by HR and Legal. Employees will be notified
of any changes at least 30 days before they take effect.
