Expense Reimbursement Policy
Last Updated: 2026-01-01
============================================================

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees submitting business
expense reimbursement requests.

## 2. Submission Deadline

All expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense
date. Reports submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed.

The 30-day submission window may be waived in special circumstances at the
discretion of Finance.

## 3. Approval Thresholds

Expenses up to $100 are approved by the employee's direct manager.
Expenses between $101 and $500 require approval from the department head.
Expenses over $500 require VP approval unless an exception is granted by
management.

## 4. Meals and Entertainment

Meal reimbursements are capped at $75 per person per meal. Alcohol is not
reimbursable under any circumstances.

## 5. Travel

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class. Hotel stays are reimbursable
up to $250 per night in tier-1 cities and $175 per night elsewhere.

International travel expenses follow standard policy except where exceptions
apply.

## 6. Receipt Requirements

Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Receipts must show the vendor
name, date, itemised amounts, and total paid. Expenses submitted without
required receipts will be automatically rejected.

## 7. Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expense reports are processed within 10 business days of final
approval. Payment is made via direct deposit to the employee's registered
bank account.
